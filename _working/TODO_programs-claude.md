## GENERAL RECOMMENDATIONS

### Across All Programs

1. **Consistency Check:**
   - Verify all programs use consistent date range formatting (YYYY–YYYY with en dash)
   - Ensure all currency values include commas (e.g., $1,000 not $1000)
   - Check that all photo credits follow "photo: Name" format

2. **Metadata Completeness:**
   - Ensure all programs have Program field populated (7 programs currently empty)
   - Verify Cover Image Credit is included for all programs
   - Add missing Video Captions where videos exist

3. **Style Consistency:**
   - Add bold opening statements to Context sections where missing (Hive, Open Badges Summit)
   - Remove empty heading tags that may have been placeholders
   - Verify all section headings follow proper hierarchy

4. **Acknowledgements:**
   - Replace all "NEED TO ADD" placeholders with actual content or remove sections
   - Research missing funders/partners from grant databases if available
   - Ensure staff lists are complete for program duration

## NOTES FOR CONTENT EDITORS

When implementing these recommendations:

1. **Preserve existing content** - These are additions/fixes, not rewrites
2. **Match existing style** - Follow patterns from complete programs (100 Days, City of Learning, Community Connections)
3. **Be consistent** - Use PROGRAM_INSTRUCTIONS.md as the style guide
4. **Verify facts** - Cross-reference with grants.csv and other source documents

## CONCLUSION

Of the 21 programs documented in PROGRAMS.md:

- **3 are exemplary** and require no changes (100 Days, City of Learning, Community Connections)
- **7 have critical issues** requiring immediate attention (missing required metadata)
- **3 have high priority issues** (incomplete content with placeholders)
- **6 have medium priority issues** (missing programs, formatting)
- **2 have low priority issues** (minor cleanup)

With focused effort in Phases 1-2 (6-9 hours), all critical and high priority issues can be resolved, bringing the full program documentation up to archival quality standards.

The most complete programs (100 Days, City of Learning, Community Connections, Belonging, Hive, One Northside, My Brother's Keeper, and Rec2Tech) demonstrate that comprehensive program documentation is achievable and should serve as models for enhancement of the remaining programs.
