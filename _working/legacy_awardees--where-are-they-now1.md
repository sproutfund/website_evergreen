Here is the retrospective research on the 16 Legacy Awardees, based on publicly available information since their 2018 recognition.

---

### **J. Thomas Agnew III**
* Continues to be a prominent cultural entrepreneur and artist advocate in Pittsburgh.
* Serves as the Co-Founder and Director of BOOM Concepts, a creative hub and gallery space that actively supports and provides opportunities for Black and Brown artists. BOOM Concepts continues to manage artist calls and community projects as of late 2025.
* Serves as the Vice-President of the Board of Directors for Shiftworks Community + Public Arts.
* **Most Recent Role:** Co-Founder/Director, BOOM Concepts; Vice-President, Shiftworks Board of Directors.

### **Zeba Ahmed**
* No significant public information found since 2018.

### **Emmai Alaquiva**
* Continues to lead his award-winning media production company, Ya Momz House, LLC, as CEO.
* Remains the Founder and Creative Director of the arts education program Hip-Hop On L.O.C.K., which he founded in 2007.
* He is a multiple Emmy Award-winning producer, director, and entrepreneur.
* **Most Recent Role:** CEO, Ya Momz House, LLC; Founder/Creative Director, Hip-Hop On L.O.C.K.

### **Annia Aleman**
* Left her role as Senior Civic Innovation Specialist with the City of Pittsburgh in November 2019.
* Joined AscenderPGH, a Pittsburgh-based business incubator, as Innovation Director in December 2019.
* Joined the Invest PGH Loan Review Committee at the Urban Redevelopment Authority of Pittsburgh (URA) in January 2024.
* Recognized in the *Pittsburgh Business Times* "30 Under 30" (2018) and "Personalities of Pittsburgh" (2023).
* **Most Recent Role:** Innovation Director, AscenderPGH.

### **Shad Ali**
* Continues to work as a music mentor and hip-hop artist in Pittsburgh.
* In September 2025, he was announced as the new head instructor for CMU's Arts Greenhouse, a community-focused hip-hop education program for youth.
* **Most Recent Role:** Head Instructor, CMU's Arts Greenhouse.

### **Ngozi Walker Tibbs**
* Continues to lead the Pittsburgh Black Breastfeeding Circle (PBBC), which she co-founded in 2014. As of early 2025, the organization is reportedly active and growing, offering support to Black mothers.
* Operates a private practice, Journey Lighter Coaching, as an Infant and Early Child Mental Health Consultant, health and relationship coach, and Master Sexpert.
* **Most Recent Role:** Co-Founder, Pittsburgh Black Breastfeeding Circle; Owner, Journey Lighter Coaching.

### **Anne Marie Toccket**
* Joined the Posner Foundation of Pittsburgh as its Executive Director in 2019.
* Maintains a role at the Philanthropy Forum at the University of Pittsburgh's Graduate School of Public and International Affairs (GSPIA).
* Serves on the boards of several nonprofit organizations, including Hello Neighbor, Grow Pittsburgh, and 5A Elite Youth Empowerment.
* **Most Recent Role:** Executive Director, Posner Foundation of Pittsburgh.

### **Heather E. Tomko**
* Crowned Ms. Wheelchair USA in 2018, shortly after the Sprout Fund recognition.
* Continues to be a prominent disability advocate, writer, and speaker.
* Runs her advocacy organization, Accessible YOUniverse, and writes a personal blog, *The Heather Report*, focusing on accessibility, representation, and life with Spinal Muscular Atrophy.
* Earned her Master of Public Health degree from the University of Pittsburgh.
* **Most Recent Role:** Founder, Accessible YOUniverse; Disability Advocate & Speaker.

### **Cecelia Ware**
* Continues to serve as the Founder and CEO of Infinite Lifestyle Solutions (ILS), a nonprofit organization focused on violence prevention, intervention, and trauma healing through restorative practices.
* Since 2018, ILS has expanded its programming, most notably with its "Saving Our Sons And Daughters" program, which provides restorative and healing circles for youth and families impacted by gun violence.
* **Most Recent Role:** Founder & CEO, Infinite Lifestyle Solutions.

### **Terri L. Baltimore**
* Continues to be an active community organizer and oral historian in the Hill District.
* She is widely known for her 30-year commitment to leading community and historical tours of the Hill District.
* In late 2024, she was nominated for the "Righteous Among the Neighbors" award by the Pittsburgh Jewish Chronicle for her work in community building and connecting the Hill District and Jewish communities.
* **Most Recent Role:** Community Organizer and Hill District Historian/Tour Guide.

### **LaKeisha Wolf**
* Continues to serve as the Executive Director of the Ujamaa Collective, a Hill District-based nonprofit and artisan boutique supporting Africana women-owned craft industries and cooperative economics.
* She serves on the board of the Hill District Federal Credit Union and the advisory board for the Pittsburgh Black Worker Center.
* Remains an active cultural worker, teaching artist, and advocate for community wellness and racial justice.
* **Most Recent Role:** Executive Director, Ujamaa Collective.

### **Adil Mansoor**
* Continues to work as a theater director and educator.
* He is a founding member and resident director with the Hatch Arts Collective.
* He is the *former* Artistic Director of Dreams of Hope (a role he held in 2018), having since moved on to focus on his personal directing and artistic projects.
* He is currently developing "Amm(i)gone," a solo performance adapting *Antigone*, which was presented in 2024 and is touring in 2025.
* He was a recipient of the 2024 Carol R. Brown Creative Achievement Award.
* **Most Recent Role:** Theater Director and Founding Member, Hatch Arts Collective.

### **Taliya Allen**
* Joined The Pittsburgh Foundation in October 2023 as a Program Associate for the Arts.
* In this role, she helps manage key initiatives, including the Advancing Black Arts in Pittsburgh (ABAP) program and the Exposure Artist Program.
* Prior to this, she served as the Director of Arts Education for 1Hood Media and as the regional manager for the Petey Greene Program, which supports the academic goals of incarcerated people.
* **Most Recent Role:** Program Associate for the Arts, The Pittsburgh Foundation.

### **Alisha B. Wormsley**
* Continues to be an internationally recognized interdisciplinary artist and cultural producer.
* Her text-based project, *There Are Black People in the Future*, gained significant prominence after its controversial removal in 2018. The work has since been exhibited and replicated in numerous cities globally.
* She founded Sibyls Shrine, a Pittsburgh-based artist residency program for Black mothers (Womxn), creatives, and activists.
* She has held roles as an adjunct professor of art and a Presidential Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Carnegie Mellon University.
* **Most Recent Role:** Interdisciplinary Artist and Founder, Sibyls Shrine.

### **Knowledge Build A. Hudson**
* Operates as a consultant and "connector" through his firm, Classic 1824 Consulting, which specializes in community engagement, promotions, and strategies.
* He is also a partner with One Blue Voice and the Epicast Network.
* His past projects (mentioned in his 2018 statement), such as the "10 Minutes of Knowledge" podcast and Unstuck PGH, remain part of his established public identity. He has more recently worked with organizations like the Kelly Strayhorn Theater on community activation.
* **Most Recent Role:** Owner, Classic 1824 Consulting.

### **Nina Marie Barbuto**
* Continues to lead Assemble, the community space for arts and technology she founded.
* Assemble remains highly active, running its flagship Afterschool STEAM program, as well as camps and workshops for children and adults in the Garfield neighborhood as of late 2025.
* **Most Recent Role:** Founder and Executive Director, Assemble.Here is the retrospective research on the 16 Legacy Awardees, based on publicly available information since their 2018 recognition.
